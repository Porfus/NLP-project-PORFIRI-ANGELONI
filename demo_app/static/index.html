DATA BALANCING METHODOLOGY (PREPARE_NEURAL_DATASET.PY)

Two-Stage Temperature Sampling ($\alpha = 0.5$, 200k Unique Sentences)

Standard Italian originally dominated 89.7% (3.66M) of the corpus. By capping it at 150k and applying power-law smoothing $p_i \propto N_i^{0.5}$, 83.5% of GPU gradients focus on the 11 dialects with zero duplicate sentences.
